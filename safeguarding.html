Safeguarding

Protecting People, Promoting Dignity and Respect

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

1. Our Commitment

Ibaji Restoration Foundation (NGO) is committed to creating an environment where people involved in our activities are treated with dignity, respect and care.

We recognize our responsibility to promote the safety and wellbeing of children, young people, vulnerable people, community members, volunteers, staff and partners who may interact with the Foundation.

🛡️

Protection

We seek to prevent abuse, exploitation, harassment and other forms of harm.

🤝

Respect

Everyone should be treated with dignity, fairness and respect.

👥

Participation

People should be able to raise concerns and participate in activities safely.

2. Who This Safeguarding Commitment Covers

Our safeguarding commitment applies to people who may participate in or interact with Foundation activities, including:

  • Children and young people
  • Vulnerable or at-risk individuals
  • Community members
  • Volunteers
  • Staff and representatives
  • Partners and participating organizations
  • Programme participants
  • Visitors and other stakeholders

3. Safeguarding Principles

The Foundation aims to apply the following principles across its activities:

  • Treat every person with dignity and respect.
  • Do not tolerate abuse, exploitation, harassment or discrimination.
  • Put the safety and wellbeing of programme participants first.
  • Take concerns seriously and respond appropriately.
  • Maintain appropriate boundaries between staff, volunteers, participants and community members.
  • Respect privacy and handle sensitive information responsibly.
  • Promote safe and inclusive participation.

4. Child Protection

Where Foundation programmes involve children or young people, additional care should be taken to protect them from abuse, exploitation, neglect and other forms of harm.

Activities involving children should be conducted in a manner appropriate to their age, wellbeing, safety and best interests.

Where required, appropriate parental, guardian or other lawful consent should be obtained.

5. Protection of Vulnerable People

The Foundation recognizes that some individuals may face greater risks because of age, disability, social circumstances, economic conditions or other factors.

We aim to take reasonable steps to reduce risks and promote safe participation for people who may be particularly vulnerable.

6. Volunteers and Staff

Volunteers, staff and representatives of the Foundation are expected to behave responsibly and respectfully when representing or participating in Foundation activities.

They should:

  • Respect programme participants and community members.
  • Maintain appropriate professional boundaries.
  • Avoid behaviour that could place another person at risk.
  • Report safeguarding concerns through appropriate channels.
  • Follow reasonable safeguarding instructions and procedures.

7. Prohibited Conduct

The Foundation does not accept conduct that involves abuse, exploitation, intimidation, harassment or deliberate harm.

Examples may include:

  • Physical abuse or violence.
  • Sexual abuse or sexual exploitation.
  • Emotional or psychological abuse.
  • Neglect where there is a duty of care.
  • Harassment, bullying or intimidation.
  • Discrimination or degrading treatment.
  • Exploitation of a person's vulnerability.
  • Using Foundation activities to facilitate harmful conduct.
Important:

Safeguarding concerns should not be ignored. If you believe someone may be at immediate risk of serious harm, contact the appropriate emergency or protection authorities in your area where necessary.

8. Reporting a Safeguarding Concern

Anyone who has a safeguarding concern relating to a Foundation activity, representative, volunteer, partner or programme should raise the concern as soon as reasonably possible.

Reports should provide factual information where possible, including what happened, when it happened and who may be affected.

People reporting concerns should avoid investigating the matter themselves or confronting an alleged offender if doing so could increase risk.

Report a Concern

If you have a safeguarding concern connected with Ibaji Restoration Foundation, please contact us through our official Contact page.

Go to Contact Page →

For emergencies involving immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency services or relevant authorities in your location.

9. Confidentiality and Privacy

Safeguarding reports should be handled sensitively. Information should only be shared with people who need it for appropriate safeguarding, legal or organizational purposes.

The Foundation will seek to respect the privacy of people involved while recognizing that confidentiality cannot always be guaranteed where disclosure is required to protect someone from harm or comply with applicable law.

10. No Retaliation

People should not be punished, threatened or intimidated simply because they raise a genuine safeguarding concern in good faith.

However, deliberately making false allegations or providing knowingly misleading information may be addressed appropriately.

11. Partnerships and Community Activities

Where the Foundation works with partner organizations, community groups or institutions, safeguarding expectations may be communicated as appropriate to the nature of the activity.

Partners may be expected to take reasonable steps to protect participants and comply with applicable safeguarding requirements.

12. Photography and Media

Photography and video involving programme participants should be handled responsibly.

Particular care should be taken when photographing or publishing images of children and vulnerable individuals.

Images should not be used in a way that exposes a person to unnecessary risk, humiliation or exploitation.

13. Online Safety

The Foundation also seeks to promote responsible online communication.

People representing the Foundation should avoid sharing private or sensitive information about programme participants without appropriate authorization.

14. Review of Safeguarding Practices

The Foundation may review and update its safeguarding practices as its programmes, partnerships and activities develop.

Where appropriate, additional safeguarding procedures, guidelines or codes of conduct may be introduced.

15. Contact

If you have questions about safeguarding or wish to report a concern relating to a Foundation activity, please use our Contact page.
