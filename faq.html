Ibaji Restoration Foundation is a non-governmental organization focused on advocating for good governance, accountability, responsible leadership, citizen participation and sustainable community development.

The Foundation seeks to encourage responsible leadership, promote accountability and transparency, strengthen citizen participation and support sustainable development within communities.
