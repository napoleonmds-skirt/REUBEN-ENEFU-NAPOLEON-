Privacy Policy

Protecting Your Information and Privacy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Ibaji Restoration Foundation (NGO) respects the privacy of visitors to our website. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit or interact with this website.

By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide through forms or other communication channels.

This may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number, if provided
  • Organization or institution name, if provided
  • Information submitted through volunteer forms
  • Information submitted through contact forms
  • Donation notification information
  • Messages or enquiries you send to us

3. How We Use Information

Information provided to the Foundation may be used for legitimate organizational purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries
  • Communicating with volunteers
  • Managing partnership enquiries
  • Responding to donor communications
  • Providing information about Foundation activities
  • Improving our website and services
  • Maintaining appropriate organizational records

4. Information You Choose to Provide

You are not required to provide personal information simply to browse publicly available pages of this website.

However, certain services, such as contacting the Foundation, volunteering or submitting a donation notification, may require you to provide relevant information.

Important:

Please do not submit passwords, bank PINs, ATM PINs, card security codes, or other highly sensitive financial credentials through ordinary website forms.

5. Donations and Payment Information

Where donation information is provided through this website, the Foundation will seek to handle it responsibly.

The Foundation does not request sensitive banking credentials such as ATM PINs, card PINs or passwords through ordinary website forms.

If third-party payment services are introduced in the future, their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.

6. Volunteers

Information submitted by individuals expressing interest in volunteering may be used to assess their suitability, communicate opportunities and coordinate legitimate volunteer activities.

7. Partners and Organizations

Information provided by organizations, institutions, community groups and development partners may be used to respond to partnership enquiries and facilitate legitimate collaboration.

8. Website Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should therefore avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through online forms.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use basic technologies such as cookies or browser storage if they are introduced to improve website functionality.

Any future third-party analytics, advertising or other services may have their own privacy practices.

10. Third-Party Websites

This website may contain links to external websites, social media platforms or other third-party services.

Ibaji Restoration Foundation is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of external websites. Users should review the privacy policies of those websites before providing personal information.

11. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children.

Where participation in a Foundation programme involves children or young people, appropriate safeguarding and consent procedures should be followed.

12. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for legitimate organizational, administrative, legal or record-keeping purposes.

When information is no longer required, reasonable steps may be taken to dispose of it appropriately.

13. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information you have provided, including the right to request access, correction or deletion of certain information.

Requests should be directed to the Foundation through the official contact information provided on our Contact page.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Ibaji Restoration Foundation may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, activities, technology or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled through this website, please contact Ibaji Restoration Foundation through our official Contact page.
