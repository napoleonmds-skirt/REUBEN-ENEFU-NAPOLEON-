Our Commitment

Ibaji Restoration Foundation (NGO) is committed to advocating for good governance and promoting responsible leadership that serves the interests of citizens and communities.

We believe that informed and engaged citizens are essential to building stronger communities and encouraging accountable leadership.

  • Good governance advocacy
  • Accountability and transparency
  • Community development
  • Citizen participation
  • Youth engagement
