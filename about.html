About Ibaji Restoration Foundation

Ibaji Restoration Foundation (NGO) is a civic and community-focused organization committed to advocating for good governance, accountability, responsible leadership and sustainable development.

The Foundation believes that communities become stronger when citizens are informed, engaged and given opportunities to participate constructively in matters that affect their lives.

Through advocacy, awareness, community engagement and development-oriented initiatives, we seek to encourage positive change and promote a culture of responsibility and accountability.

Our work is guided by respect for people, community participation, transparency and the pursuit of sustainable solutions.

Our Objectives

Key areas through which the Foundation seeks to achieve its mission.

What We Seek to Achieve

  • Promote good governance and responsible leadership.
  • Encourage transparency and accountability in public affairs.
  • Create awareness about citizens' roles and responsibilities.
  • Encourage constructive community dialogue and participation.
