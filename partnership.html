OUR PARTNERS

Working together with organizations, institutions, community groups and development partners to promote good governance and sustainable community development.

Partnership & Collaboration

Strong partnerships can help communities achieve sustainable and meaningful development.

Ibaji Restoration Foundation recognises the importance of collaboration in advancing good governance, accountability, citizen participation and community development.

We welcome constructive relationships with organizations, institutions, community groups, professional bodies, development partners and individuals who share compatible values and objectives.

Who We Work With

Our partnership approach is open to organizations and groups that share our commitment to positive community development.

🏢

Organizations

Civil society organizations, non-governmental organizations and other relevant bodies.

🏫

Institutions

Educational, professional and community institutions that support learning and development.

🤝

Community Groups

Community associations, youth groups and other grassroots organizations.

🌍

Development Partners

Partners interested in supporting sustainable development and community initiatives.
